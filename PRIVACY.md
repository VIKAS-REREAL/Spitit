# Spitit Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

Spitit is designed with a strict **local-first, zero-telemetry architecture**. We believe in radical transparency: your voice and your data belong to you. This policy explains plainly what leaves your machine, what stays local, and how your data is handled.

---

## 1. Summary of Principles

- **Zero REREAL Servers:** There are no Spitit or REREAL backend servers, databases, or accounts.
- **Zero Telemetry or Tracking:** Spitit contains no analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or background telemetry pings.
- **Direct Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK):** Audio streams directly between your machine and Groq Cloud over encrypted TLS using your personal API key.
- **Zero Unintended Keystroke Logging:** The keyboard hook only monitors configured hotkeys and never stores, records, or transmits what you type.

---

## 2. What Leaves Your Computer

When you press your dictation hotkey and speak:

1. **Audio Data**: Your microphone captures audio strictly while the hotkey is active. Raw audio is converted in-memory into 16 kHz mono WAV samples and streamed directly to **Groq Cloud API** (`api.groq.com`) over HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
2. **Your Personal API Key**: Your Groq API key is included in the HTTP Authorization header sent directly to Groq.
3. **Third-Party Processing**: Your audio is processed under Groq's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. REREAL never receives, intercepts, or proxies this audio.
   - [Groq Privacy Policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)
   - [Groq Acceptable Use Policy](https://groq.com/acceptable-use-policy/)

**Nothing else ever leaves your computer.**

---

## 3. What Stays Strictly on Your Computer

1. **Groq API Key**: Saved securely in the native **Windows Credential Manager** (`Generic Credential` target `Spitit`). It is never written in plaintext to settings files or SQLite.
2. **Text History**: If enabled in Settings, past transcriptions are saved locally in an embedded SQLite database (`%APPDATA%\com.rereal.spitit\spitit.db`). You can disable history at any time; when disabled, transcriptions exist solely in the Windows Clipboard buffer and zero text is written to disk.
3. **Usage Statistics**: Word counts, dictation duration (seconds), and calculated WPM are logged locally in SQLite to display your personal productivity dashboard.
4. **Foreground Window Metadata**: Spitit queries the active window title and process icon (`GetForegroundWindow` / `GetWindowThreadProcessId`) solely to display the current application icon inside the status pill and track per-app usage stats locally. **Spitit does not read window content, document text, or background processes.**
5. **Application Settings**: Hotkey preferences, UI mode, audio thresholds, and language preferences are stored locally in the local SQLite database.

---

## 4. What the Keyboard Hook Does & Does Not Do

To enable global hotkeys across all Windows applications, Spitit registers a Win32 Low-Level Keyboard Hook (`WH_KEYBOARD_LL`):

- **Does**: Monitors physical key-down and key-up state for designated hotkeys (e.g., `Alt + Left Shift`) to start and stop dictation.
- **Does NOT**: Record, log, buffer, or inspect text typed into any application.
- **Does NOT**: Transmit keystroke data to any remote server.
- **Does NOT**: Swallow, intercept, or modify keystrokes outside the configured hotkey combination.

The complete hook implementation is open source and auditable in [`src-tauri/src/hotkey.rs`](file:///d:/codevs/Spitit/src-tauri/src/hotkey.rs).

---

## 5. Website Privacy

The Spitit project website (`https://spitit.in`) is a static website:
- **No Cookies**: The website sets zero tracking cookies and uses no session trackers.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or ad tracking scripts are loaded.
- **Self-Hosted Assets**: Fonts and media are loaded locally or from privacy-respecting CDNs without passing personal data.

---

## 6. How to Delete All Your Data

You have complete control over all data generated by Spitit:

1. **Clear Dictation History**: In Spitit Settings -> History & Privacy, click **"Clear History Now"**.
2. **Disable Text Saving**: Toggle off **"Save History in Application"** to prevent any future transcription from touching disk.
3. **Remove Stored API Key**: In Settings -> Groq API Key, delete your key, or open Windows Credential Manager (`control keymgr.dll`) -> Windows Credentials -> locate `Spitit` and click **Remove**.
4. **Complete Data Removal**: Delete the local application directory:  
   `%APPDATA%\com.rereal.spitit\`  
   Uninstall Spitit via Windows **Installed Apps**.

---

## 7. Responsible User Use & Consent

Because Spitit captures live audio from your system microphone, you are responsible for ensuring that:
- You comply with applicable local, state, and national laws regarding audio recording and two-party consent if dictating in environments where other individuals are speaking.
- You do not dictate confidential, classified, or sensitive corporate information without checking your organization's third-party cloud AI policies regarding Groq APIs.

---

## 8. Regional Compliance Notes (GDPR & DPDP Act)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023):
- Spitit acts as client-side software running on your personal hardware.
- Because Spitit and REREAL collect, store, and process **zero personal data on remote servers**, REREAL is not a Data Controller or Data Processor for your dictation audio.
- When you use your own Groq API key, you establish a direct contractual and data relationship with Groq Inc. as the data processor of your audio streams.
- If you have questions regarding data privacy hygiene, contact us via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/VIKAS-REREAL/Spitit/discussions).
