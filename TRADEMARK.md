## 2. Permitted Uses (What You May Do)

We encourage open-source forks and community contributions. You may:

- **Fork and modify the source code:** Build and run custom versions on your own machine.
- **State factual origin:** Say that your fork or software is *"Based on Spitit"*, *"Derived from Spitit source code"*, or *"A fork of the Spitit project"*.
- **Link back:** Include hyperlinks to the official GitHub repository (`https://github.com/VIKAS-REREAL/Spitit`) and website (`https://spitit.in`).
- **Submit Pull Requests:** Contribute enhancements or bug fixes to the official repository.

---

## 3. Restricted Uses (What You May Not Do)

Without prior written permission from the project maintainer, you may **not**:

- **Distribute binaries or installers under the Spitit or REREAL name:** If you compile, distribute, or package a release (e.g., on GitHub Releases, Microsoft Store, Winget, or third-party download portals), you must rename your software and use a distinct project name.
- **Use the official logos or app icons:** Your distributed builds and listings must use your own original app icons and branding.
- **Imply official endorsement or affiliation:** You may not represent your fork, website, or service as an official Spitit release, or imply endorsement or sponsorship by REREAL.
- **Register confusingly similar domain names or social handles:** Do not register domains or accounts containing the Spitit name to distribute third-party builds or services.

---

## 4. Why This Policy Exists

Because Spitit installs a low-level global keyboard hook and interfaces directly with audio capture APIs and user credentials, security and trust are paramount. If an unofficial build containing malicious modifications were distributed under the Spitit name, users would face security risks and the project's reputation would be damaged. This policy protects users and the community by guaranteeing that software bearing the Spitit name comes exclusively from official, audited releases.

## 5. Trademark Availability & Legal Notes

### Notice Regarding Trademark Status
- The names "Spitit" and "REREAL" are common-law brand names and trade dress established through continuous public use.
- As of this version, formal registration is pending. The names are not claimed as registered trademarks (which would use the ® symbol). The ™ symbol signifies an unregistered common-law trademark claim, whereas the ® symbol is legally restricted to trademarks officially registered with a government trademark office.
- Anyone establishing or commercializing forks is strongly advised to conduct trademark conflict clearance.
